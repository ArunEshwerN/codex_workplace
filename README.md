## 8. Pull-Up Return Block



Use this block until the right delt/top-shoulder issue is fully settled.



**Pain-free screen before every pull-up session:** full overhead reach, arm circles, scapular pull-up motion, and a 10–15s assisted hang. If any of these hurt, skip pull-ups and do gentle shoulder mobility only.



**Deloaded assisted session:** assisted scapular pulls `2×5`, high-assistance pull-ups `3×3–5`, assisted negatives `2×3` at 3–5 seconds. No unbanded negatives, no max holds, no failure.



**Stop rule:** stop immediately if pain is sharp, above `2/10`, changes your movement, or returns later that day. If shoulder pain persists or worsens for 2 weeks, get medical/physio help. Reference: [NHS shoulder pain guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shoulder-pain/) and [AAOS shoulder pain guidance](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/diseases--conditions/shoulder-pain-and-common-shoulder-problems?webid=2FDEE455).



**Progression rule:** reduce assistance only after 2 consecutive pain-free sessions where all reps are smooth, shoulders stay level, and there is no next-day ache. Do not return to unbanded negatives until you can do assisted pull-ups `3×6–8`, assisted negatives `3×3` at 6–8 seconds, and top holds `2×8–10s` without shoulder shift or pain.

## 8. Pull-Up Return Block



Use this block until the right delt/top-shoulder issue is fully settled.



**Pain-free screen before every pull-up session:** full overhead reach, arm circles, scapular pull-up motion, and a 10–15s assisted hang. If any of these hurt, skip pull-ups and do gentle shoulder mobility only.



**Deloaded assisted session:** assisted scapular pulls `2×5`, high-assistance pull-ups `3×3–5`, assisted negatives `2×3` at 3–5 seconds. No unbanded negatives, no max holds, no failure.



**Stop rule:** stop immediately if pain is sharp, above `2/10`, changes your movement, or returns later that day. If shoulder pain persists or worsens for 2 weeks, get medical/physio help. Reference: [NHS shoulder pain guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shoulder-pain/) and [AAOS shoulder pain guidance](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/diseases--conditions/shoulder-pain-and-common-shoulder-problems?webid=2FDEE455).



**Progression rule:** reduce assistance only after 2 consecutive pain-free sessions where all reps are smooth, shoulders stay level, and there is no next-day ache. Do not return to unbanded negatives until you can do assisted pull-ups `3×6–8`, assisted negatives `3×3` at 6–8 seconds, and top holds `2×8–10s` without shoulder shift or pain.
