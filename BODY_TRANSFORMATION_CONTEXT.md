## 7. Pull-Up Technique Rules

### Grip

- Use a normal overhand grip around shoulder width or slightly wider.
- Thumb should wrap the bar if comfortable; do not use a loose fingertip-only grip.
- Set the hand so the bar sits securely across the base of the fingers/palm, then close fingers and thumb.
- Avoid death-gripping so hard that the forearms dominate, but do not let the hand slide.

### Shoulder position

- At the very bottom of a relaxed dead hang, shoulders naturally rise closer to the ears.
- During working reps, do **not** stay jammed into the ears.
- Think: shoulder blades gently down/back, chest proud, neck long, ribs controlled.
- Do not overdo scapular depression before every rep so much that the movement becomes stiff and delayed.

### Pulling cue

Best simple cue: **“Chest/ribs toward the bar; elbows toward the ribs.”**

Avoid overthinking:

- Do not separately pull shoulder blades down, pause, then try to pull with arms/hips.
- Do not crane the neck to “peck” the bar.
- Do not turn the pull-up into a body row by leaning way back.
- A slight backward lean is okay.

### Negative cue

- Get to the top with help if needed.
- Hold about 1 second.
- Lower evenly for the assigned seconds.
- Stop the set if the right shoulder takes over, the body twists, or pain appears.

## 8. Stop Rules

Stop the pull-up work immediately if any of these happen:

- Sharp pain.
- Pain above **2/10**.
- Pain changes the movement.
- Right shoulder load shift becomes obvious and uncontrollable.
- Pain returns later that day or the next day.
- Clicking/noise plus pain or instability.

If shoulder pain persists, worsens, or keeps returning for two weeks despite deloading, get assessed by a qualified clinician/physiotherapist.

## 12. Dumbbell Progression System

Use double progression.

1. Pick a load where the low end of the rep range is challenging but clean.
2. Keep that load until all sets reach the top of the rep range.
3. Increase load by the smallest practical jump.
4. Drop back toward the low end of the rep range.
5. Build up again.

Examples:

- For 3 x 8-12: increase only after all three sets hit 12 with clean form.
- For 3 x 12-15: increase only after all three sets hit 15 with clean form.
- For small lifts, use smaller jumps if available. If not, progress reps, tempo, and control before load.
